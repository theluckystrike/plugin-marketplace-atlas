Checked 2026-11-09. Every row links to its source. F = read on the store's own page or API that day; E = estimate or search snippet; reported = third-party page.

Free sample: fee and review time only. The full Atlas adds payout methods, minimums, timing, merchant of record, sign-up steps, paid-listing rules and the non-Stripe payout check for every store.
